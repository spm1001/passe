The grammar was overhauled in March 2026 to match what Claudes naturally write. Three verbs collapsed into one (`wait` now handles sleep, selector, and network idle based on argument shape), `read` was renamed to `extract` (with `read` as hidden alias), `click` handles both selectors and text natively (no `click-text` needed), `screenshot` path became optional, and the `-c` flag for inline scripts became optional. Dead verbs `click-if` and `wait-navigation` were removed (zero usage across 1,199 real invocations). All timeouts are seconds — no milliseconds anywhere in the user-facing API. The internal functions `do_wait_for` and `do_wait_idle` now take `timeout: float` (seconds) not `timeout_ms: int`. The design principle: if every Claude writes it a certain way unprompted, that's the right grammar.
