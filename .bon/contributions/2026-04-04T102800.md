ax-tree --compact's real value is signal density, not node count. On 8 live sites, compact strips 27-74% of nodes (text leaves), but the important metric is that actionable node density jumps from ~12% to 18-45%. Wikipedia goes from 12% actionable to 45%. Text-heavy pages (Wikipedia, HN, GOV.UK) benefit most; structure-heavy sites (Guardian, BBC) barely change. The <100 node target from the original brief was unrealistic and the wrong metric — compact makes trees useful for navigation, not small.
