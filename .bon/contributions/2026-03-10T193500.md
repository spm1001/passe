Clean-environment Claude survey (n=43, 10 Mar 2026) revealed that passe's existing verb vocabulary is closer to model instincts than expected — `goto` (81%) and `wait` (100%) are already the natural choices. But three verb mismatches are clear: Claudes universally write `click "text"` not `click-text` (0/43 used the hyphenated form), `extract` not `read` for content extraction (10/10), and `screenshot` bare without a path (53%). Wait timing is strongly seconds-biased (81% wrote seconds, 19% ms). Tabs are universally assumed to persist between invocations. 30% of Claudes skip the `run` subcommand entirely. The survey methodology required physically hiding `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` (the `--system-prompt` flag does NOT suppress it), using `--tools ""` (not `--allowed-tools ""`), and a fictional tool name to prevent training data contamination. Subagents spawned via the Agent tool inherit full project context including CLAUDE.md and can discover SKILL.md files by globbing — they cannot be isolated for this kind of research.
