[17/33] From: Ella Collis | Date: 21 Oct 2025, 14:34:37

Hi Sarah

Apologies for the delay in getting back to you.

I have gone through the agreement and responded to your suggested changes. Having reviewed those changes and your 25 Sept email regarding the need for row-level data for retargeting, it seems we have a genuine misunderstanding about the fundamental purpose and required compliance model for this Clean Room collaboration.

To be absolutely clear, the Clean Room is intended for aggregated measurement and insights, not for the transfer of individual-level data for external activation or retargeting.

We cannot proceed with a structure that requires row-level data output because ITV’s legal basis and our data governance documents (including our Privacy Notice) do not permit the sharing of individual, row-level exposure data (even matched via email or IP) with a third party for activation purposes. This poses a severe and unacceptable regulatory liability risk for ITV under GDPR.

The security and privacy design of the Clean Room also dictates that all sensitive matching and analysis must occur internally, releasing only fully aggregated, non-individualised insights. Therefore, we must insist that all queries and analysis be performed strictly within the Clean Room environment, and the only data released to either Party is the final, fully aggregated, non-identifiable report.

We require the Expected Outputs clause to revert to our previous language, which explicitly prohibits row-level data and retargeting.

We are happy to jump on a call to clarify the reasons behind this requirement, but the data security position, which is driven by our published legal commitments, is non-negotiable for our legal team.
